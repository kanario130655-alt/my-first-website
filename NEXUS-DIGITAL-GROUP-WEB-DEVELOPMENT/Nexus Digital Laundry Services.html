Our Story

Pioneering Modern Eco-Laundry

Founded by the Nexus Digital team, we are modernizing garment care with zero-emission washing practices and sustainable solutions.

Revolutionizing Garment Care Since 2024

Nexus Digital Laundry Services was established with a single vision: removing the hassle from weekly laundry while protecting our environment.

Traditional dry cleaners use harsh chemical solvents like PERC. At Nexus Digital, we utilize 100% wet-cleaning technology and gentle, non-toxic detergents that leave garments softer, cleaner, and scent-fresh without harming water systems.

Our Core Commitments:

  • Zero perchloroethylene (PERC-free) cleaning formulas
  • Water recycling filtration systems saving 40% water per wash
  • Reusable garment bags for every recurring subscriber
Fabric Glossary

Understanding Our Specialized Care

Learn how we treat different garments in our modern facility.

Wet Cleaning Technology
A non-toxic, eco-friendly alternative to dry cleaning that uses water and specialized biodegradable detergents to safely clean delicate fabrics including silk, wool, and rayon.
Steam Pressing & Finishing
High-temperature steam treatment that eliminates 99.9% of bacteria while removing stubborn wrinkles from dress shirts, suits, and trousers.
Wash & Fold (By the Pound)
Everyday laundry service where clothes are sorted by color, washed in temperature-controlled water, tumble dried on low heat, and neatly folded.
Sanitizing Ozone Treatment
An advanced wash cycle enriched with active oxygen that neutralizes deep odors, bacteria, and allergens without damaging delicate fabric fibers.
